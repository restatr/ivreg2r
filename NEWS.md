# ivreg2r (development version)

* Bundled four new datasets used throughout the help-file examples: `klein`
  (Klein 1950 Model I macro data, for LIML/CUE/Fuller/k-class examples;
  drops the upstream's precomputed `profits1`/`totinc1` lag columns since
  the `l()` time-series operator now computes them directly), `grunfeld`
  (Grunfeld investment panel, for HAC/cluster/Driscoll-Kraay examples),
  `abdata` (Arellano-Bond UK employment panel, for GMM2S/cluster/Kiefer
  examples), and `nlswork` (NLS Young Women panel, for one- and two-way
  cluster examples).
